01 A Different Way of Thinking

Dev Saholiya believes that financial markets should not be a battlefield where only institutions with massive resources, advanced infrastructure, and sophisticated technology have an advantage.

For decades, major financial institutions, investment banks, hedge funds, and professional trading firms have invested enormous resources into understanding market behaviour, liquidity, execution, and systematic trading.

Meanwhile, millions of independent traders are often left relying on conventional indicators, delayed information, emotional decisions, and strategies they barely understand.

Dev believes this technological gap deserves to be challenged. His vision is not to fight institutions blindly, but to understand the systems, research the principles behind their operations, and develop intelligent algorithmic tools that bring professional-level thinking closer to independent traders.

02 Challenging the Existing Boundaries

One of Dev Saholiya's strongest beliefs is that access to advanced trading technology should not be determined by the size of someone's capital.

A trader operating independently should have the opportunity to explore systematic approaches, understand market structure, study liquidity behaviour, and make decisions through logic rather than emotion.

He questions conventional trading approaches:

  • Why should traders remain dependent on lagging indicators?
  • Why should sophisticated market analysis remain inaccessible to independent developers?
  • Why should trading decisions depend so heavily on human emotions?
  • Why can't algorithmic systems continuously evolve through research, testing, and statistical evidence?
  • Why should technology become a barrier instead of an opportunity?

These questions form the foundation of his development philosophy.

03 Beyond Indicators. Beyond Conventional Trading.

Dev's approach to algorithmic development focuses on understanding the market rather than simply reacting to price movements.

He believes that price is only one part of the story. Behind every movement exists market participation, liquidity, execution, volatility, and changing market conditions.

His long-term research vision revolves around:

Institutional-style market structure analysis
Liquidity behaviour and price discovery
Order flow and execution intelligence
Quantitative research and statistical validation
Adaptive algorithmic systems
Risk management and performance consistency
Continuous optimization without blindly curve-fitting historical data

The objective is to develop systems that are built on research, measurable logic, and disciplined execution.

04 The Philosophy of SWARAJ ALGO'S

SWARAJ represents independence.

For Dev Saholiya, the name carries a deeper meaning: financial technology should empower independent minds rather than make them permanently dependent on traditional systems.

Swaraj Algo's was founded around the idea that innovation should not belong exclusively to the biggest players.

The mission is to research, develop, and deliver algorithmic solutions that encourage independent traders to think differently, approach markets systematically, and explore the possibilities of modern trading technology.

It is not about promising overnight wealth. It is about building better systems, understanding risk, learning from failures, and improving through evidence.

05 A Long-Term Vision

Dev does not see algorithmic trading as a finished industry. He sees it as an evolving field where research, mathematics, computing power, market data, and intelligent automation will continue to redefine how financial markets are approached.

His ambition is to build an ecosystem of advanced trading technologies that challenges conventional limitations and makes sophisticated systematic trading more accessible.

Every strategy is an experiment. Every backtest is a source of information. Every failure is an opportunity to understand what the market is teaching.

Because the objective is not simply to create a profitable algorithm today, but to develop a research-driven foundation capable of evolving with tomorrow's markets.

The biggest advantage in financial markets should not simply be who has the most money, the biggest institution, or the most expensive infrastructure. Knowledge, research, technology, and disciplined execution should create opportunities for independent minds too.
